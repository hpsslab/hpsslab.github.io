Lab director

Mehmet E. Belviranli

Associate Professor of Computer Science
Colorado School of Mines

Mehmet directs HPSS Lab. His research focuses on heterogeneous architectures, performance and resource modeling, runtime systems, and autonomous computing.

He received his Ph.D. from the University of California, Riverside in 2016 and worked at Oak Ridge National Laboratory before joining Mines in 2019.
