Join the lab

We welcome interest from Ph.D., master’s, and undergraduate students who want to understand how computing systems work and improve how they use resources.

Research interest form ↗{{ site.email }}

Research topics

Start with a research area that connects to your interests. Our projects span hardware measurement, analytical modeling, optimization, runtime software, and application experiments.

  • AI Acceleration
  • Heterogeneous and edge computing
  • Resource-aware scheduling for robots
  • Memory systems, security, and architecture simulation
  • Computer vision and interactive cleanroom training

Contact

Use the research interest form linked from Mehmet Belviranli’s website. A useful introduction explains your degree level, relevant coursework or project experience, and the research problem you want to explore. Links to code, a project report, or a CV can provide context.

You can also contact Mehmet Belviranli by email.

Degree programs

Ph.D. students

Read a recent paper and identify how its methods relate to your interests. For degree requirements and the application process, consult the Mines Computer Science Department and Graduate Admissions.

Master’s students

If you are considering thesis research, share your interests and the time you can commit. Current Mines students can contact the lab to discuss a possible project.

Undergraduate students

Tell us about relevant classes, programming experience, and a topic you would like to study. A class project or independent implementation can help start the discussion.

Before you contact us

Getting started

  1. Browse the research areas.
  2. Read one of our recent papers.
  3. Describe a problem that interests you and what you have worked on.
